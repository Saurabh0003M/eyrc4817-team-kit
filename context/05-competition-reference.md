# Competition reference — rules, schedule, resources

Lookup only. Nothing here is needed day-to-day; `CLAUDE.md` carries the operative facts. Verbatim
source text lives in `../_extracted/Competition_Info__*.txt`.

## Rules that actually constrain us

- **Roster is frozen.** No member may be changed or replaced after registration. A minimum of 2
  members must be maintained throughout.
- **One email, one team.** Each member registers with exactly one email ID and must sign in with
  that exact address or activation fails.
- **Same college** for all members; branch and year may differ.
- **Two themes in Task 0, one theme later.** The schedule doc says commitment starts from Task 2;
  the rules doc says Task 3. **The source documents disagree — confirm on the portal.**
- **e-Yantra makes the final theme allocation.** Teams express a preference, they do not self-assign.
- **Kits are returnable** and go only to Stage-2 selected teams.
- **Plagiarism means disqualification.** All e-Yantra material remains e-Yantra's IP; do not
  redistribute it.
- Participation consents to name and photograph use for publicity.

## RoboReady (optional)

Free, voluntary, five-hour online collaborative-problem-solving course, part of an IIT Bombay
research programme. Four sequential stages, each with team tasks and submitted artifacts; every
member receives a letter of completion. Register by emailing the team leader's address to
`suprabhaj@iitb.ac.in`; credentials arrive within 24 hours. **Stage 4 requires a Firebird V robot** —
check college availability, or skip that stage. Needs all four members together for the full session.

## FAQ answers worth knowing

- No robotics background is fine; e-Yantra provides instruction from scratch.
- Multiple teams from one college may compete, but must work completely independently.
- Stage 1 runs as a self-paced MOOC on open-source software.
- Internship eligibility requires being among the top performers.
